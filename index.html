About

My name is Petra Dohnalova and I am a registered dietitian and a lifestyle medicine practitioner. You might be wondering: What exactly is a registered dietitian? A Registered Dietitian (RD or RDN) is a board-certified food and nutrition expert who provides personalized, evidence-based dietary guidance to improve health and manage medical conditions.

Now that we’ve got that covered, I can also share that I am a passionate foodie. Why does that matter? Because working with someone who loves food has its perks — I explore global cuisines for fun, so eating healthy never gets boring. I want your meals to be not just nutritious, but also delicious and visually appealing. If you’re a fellow foodie, you know exactly what I mean.

Lastly, I’m also a lifelong athlete. No, I haven’t competed in the Olympics, but I’ve stayed active through all of life’s ups and downs — I know firsthand how movement supports both physical health and mental well-being.

I share all this because I don’t just teach healthy living — I live it. I’ve spent years learning, practicing, and refining what works, and I’ll guide you toward better health and well-being without the trial and error. It doesn’t have to take a lifetime, and you don’t have to figure it out alone — I’m here to help you do it smarter, not harder.

While eating well and staying active matter — so do sleep, stress, and your support system. As a lifestyle medicine practitioner, I check in on all these areas because they each play a role in how you feel day to day and how well your body and mind function. Humans are complex, and that’s why we’ll take a well-rounded approach — together, we’ll make sure all the important pieces are in place so you can truly thrive, not just survive!

What exactly is a registered dietitian?

Registered Dietitians use evidence-based science to help people make healthier choices. They are qualified, regulated professionals you can trust for credible, individualized nutrition guidance.

Registered Dietitians:

  • Have completed a college degree in nutrition, dietetics, or a related field.
  • Have completed supervised practical training (often called an internship or supervised practice).
  • Have passed a national exam to earn the RD credential
  • Keep their knowledge up to date through ongoing education.

What do they do?

  • Assess people’s nutritional needs and health goals.
  • Provide personalized nutrition advice for managing diseases (like diabetes, heart disease, or kidney problems).
  • Help with weight management and healthy eating.
  • Guide patients with food allergies or intolerances.
  • Work in hospitals, clinics, community health programs, research, food service, or private practice.

What is a lifestyle medicine practitioner?

A Diplomate of the American College of Lifestyle Medicine (DipACLM) is a board-certified professional who can help you prevent, treat, and even reverse chronic diseases by guiding you in evidence-based lifestyle changes such as improving nutrition, increasing physical activity, managing stress, optimizing sleep, and avoidance of harmful substances.

Do you provide virtual or telehealth consultations?

Yes! All of my sessions are virtual, so you can connect with me from the comfort of your home or even while traveling—making it easy to stay on track no matter where you are.

Can you help with specific health conditions?

Yes! Registered dietitians are uniquely qualified—alongside physicians—to provide safe, evidence-based nutrition guidance for various health conditions. Because incorrect advice can cause harm, it’s important to always verify the credentials of your healthcare providers. In many states, only licensed professionals like RDs are legally allowed to provide medical nutrition therapy.

What should I expect during my first visit?

Your first visit is an exploratory session where we get to know each other, discuss your health goals, and clarify expectations. This helps lay the foundation for a supportive, long-term partnership tailored to your needs.
