About

My name is Petra Dohnalova, and I’m a Board-certified Registered Dietitian Nutritionist (RDN) and a Lifestyle Medicine Practitioner. With extensive experience working in clinics and hospitals, I now focus on providing virtual nutrition counseling and online group classes designed to fit seamlessly into your busy lifestyle.

You might be wondering: What exactly is a registered dietitian? An RDN is a board-certified food and nutrition expert who provides personalized, evidence-based dietary guidance to improve health and manage medical conditions.

Now that we’ve got that covered, here’s a little more about me — I’m a passionate foodie. Why does that matter? Because working with someone who loves food means your meals will never be boring. I explore global cuisines for fun, so I know how to make healthy eating delicious, satisfying, and visually appealing. If you’re a fellow foodie, you know exactly what I mean.

I’m also a lifelong athlete. I may not have competed in the Olympics, but I’ve stayed active through all of life’s ups and downs, and I know firsthand how movement supports not only physical health but also mental well-being.

I share this because I don’t just teach healthy living — I live it. After years of learning, practicing, and refining what works, I’m here to guide you toward better health and well-being without all the guesswork. It doesn’t have to take a lifetime, and you don’t have to do it alone — I’ll help you work smarter, not harder.

Are you a busy professional who feels exhausted, overwhelmed, or stuck with early health issues like high blood pressure, prediabetes, or low energy? Do you already have chronic conditions that you are trying to manage? You want to feel better, but complicated diets or extreme lifestyle changes don’t fit your life. I get it — and I’m here to help.

I specialize in a plant-forward approach that fits your lifestyle — no pressure to go fully vegan, just flexible, practical steps that increase your energy, improve your health, and are sustainable for you.

Here’s what I can do for you:

  • Show you how to eat more plants and whole foods in ways that fit your busy schedule and taste preferences.
  • Support you with lifestyle medicine strategies — nutrition, sleep, stress, movement, and more — because your health depends on the whole picture.
  • Help you reverse or manage chronic conditions like type 2 diabetes, hypertension, and high cholesterol with personalized, realistic plans.
  • Guide young adults transitioning to vegan or plant-based diets, making sure they get all the nutrients they need confidently.
  • Work with those managing long-term health issues to improve quality of life through compassionate, achievable strategies.

All my services are virtual — individual counseling and group classes — so you get expert support wherever you are, on your schedule.

If you’re ready to stop feeling tired and overwhelmed and start feeling vibrant, energized, and in control of your health, I’m ready to help you make it happen.

FAQ

What exactly does a Registered Dietitian Nutritionist (RDN) do, and how can you help me?

Great question! As a Board-certified Registered Dietitian Nutritionist and Lifestyle Medicine practitioner, I’m an expert in food and nutrition and lifestyle medicine with evidence-based training to guide you through personalized changes that fit your life. I don’t just hand you a diet plan — I help you figure out what works for you, your body, and your busy life. Whether you want more energy, to manage a health condition, or simply feel better day to day, I’ll guide you step-by-step with flexible, realistic changes that feel doable—not overwhelming.

Registered Dietitians:

  • Have completed a college degree in nutrition, dietetics, or a related field.
  • Have completed supervised practical training.
  • Have passed a national exam to earn the RD credential.
  • Keep their knowledge and licensure up to date through lifelong ongoing education.
  • Assess people’s nutritional needs and health goals.
  • Provide personalized nutrition advice and medical nutrition therapy for managing diseases (like diabetes, heart disease, or kidney problems).
  • Help with weight management and healthy eating.
  • Guide patients with food allergies or intolerances.
  • Work in hospitals, clinics, community health programs, research, food service, or private practices.

What is a Lifestyle Medicine Practitioner?

As a Diplomate of the American College of Lifestyle Medicine (DipACLM), I’m board-certified to help prevent, treat, and in some cases reverse chronic diseases through evidence-based lifestyle changes. We’ll work together on all six pillars of lifestyle medicine — nutrition, physical activity, restorative sleep, stress management, avoidance of risky substances, and positive social connections — which are scientifically proven to work in synergy to improve health, boost energy, and enhance quality of life.

I’m exhausted and overwhelmed. How can nutrition and lifestyle medicine really help me?

I hear you — feeling tired all the time is frustrating and can make even simple tasks feel impossible. That’s why I don’t just focus on food alone. As a Lifestyle Medicine Practitioner with a Master’s in Integrative Nutrition, I look at all the pieces that affect your well-being: nutrition, sleep, stress, movement, and your support system. When we address these together, you’ll start noticing real shifts in your energy and mood. You don’t have to make extreme changes or go 100% vegan; small, sustainable steps tailored to your life make the biggest difference.

I have a chronic health condition that’s been around for years. Can you help me improve my quality of life?

Yes. While long-standing conditions may not always be completely reversible, there’s so much we can do to improve how you feel day-to-day and boost your quality of life. I take a compassionate, realistic approach that combines plant-forward nutrition with lifestyle medicine strategies customized to your unique needs. Together, we’ll focus on achievable changes that support your health, energy, and well-being — so you’re not just managing, but thriving.

Do I have to go 100% vegan to work with you? I still eat meat, eggs, and dairy.

Not at all. I specialize in a plant-forward approach, which means plants are the focus, but you don’t have to cut out animal products completely. This way, you can make healthier choices that fit your tastes and lifestyle—without feeling restricted or stressed about being “perfect.”

I want to try a plant-based or vegan diet but don’t know where to start. Can you help?

Absolutely! Transitioning to a plant-based lifestyle can feel confusing or overwhelming, especially with so much conflicting information out there. I’m a passionate foodie who loves exploring global cuisines, so I’ll help you discover meals that are not only nutritious but delicious and exciting. I’ll guide your transition to an upgraded style of eating from A to Z to make sure you get all the nutrients you need and help you build confidence in your new lifestyle without pressure to be perfect.

I’m young and thinking about going vegan but I am/my parents are worried about missing nutrients. Can you help me?

Yes! I also specialize in guiding young adults new to vegan or plant-based eating to get all the nutrients they need safely and confidently, without confusion or overwhelm. I’ve walked this path myself — and learned the hard way — so you can skip the confusion and start thriving from day one.

How do your virtual sessions work? Can I fit this into my busy schedule?

Yes! All my counseling and group classes are 100% virtual and designed with busy professionals in mind. You get flexible, personalized support whenever and wherever it works for you — no wasted time, no unnecessary hassle.

Why choose you instead of trying to figure all this out on my own?

I’ve been exactly where you are — overwhelmed by health advice and tired of trial and error. With my combination of credentials (RDN, Lifestyle Medicine Diplomate, Integrative Nutrition MS), years of clinical experience, and a passion for real food and movement, I bring a whole-person approach that simplifies your journey. You don’t have to do this alone or “go hard” to see results. I’ll help you make smarter, sustainable choices that lead to real, lasting improvements in your energy and health.

What makes your approach different from other dietitians or nutrition coaches?

I combine clinical expertise with a real-life approach — backed by credentials like being Board-certified, a Lifestyle Medicine Diplomate, and holding a Master’s in Integrative Nutrition. Plus, I’m a fellow busy professional and lifelong athlete who loves food. I get how hard it is to make health a priority, and I focus on creating sustainable habits that energize you, without guilt or burnout.

How soon will I see results?

While everyone’s journey is unique, many clients notice more energy, better mood, and positive health changes within 8-12 weeks of making manageable lifestyle shifts. I focus on creating habits that last, so your improvements stick long-term.

Your first session is an open, exploratory conversation where we get to know each other, discuss your goals, challenges, and what you want to achieve. This sets the foundation for a supportive, personalized partnership focused on making meaningful and sustainable changes that fit your busy life.
